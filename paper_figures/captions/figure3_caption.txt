Both heatmaps use identical position axes, A/C/G/T channel order, and color normalization. (C) Phase 2D Exact ISM normalized L1 across fold-by-seed runs on a linear scale; CNN-RCPS values are near numerical zero. (D) Frozen Phase 2F CNN-Raw H1b estimate and 95% CI among prediction-consistent observations. Near-identical predictions can coexist with substantial attribution asymmetry.
